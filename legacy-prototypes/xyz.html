Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for motorcycle rentals?

You will need a valid original Driving License, a government ID proof (Aadhaar Card or Passport), and a security deposit amount depending on the bike model.

Do you provide helmets and safety gear?

Yes, rider helmets are provided with every rental. Riding jackets, knee pads, and gloves are also available on request.

What happens in case of a breakdown during the trip?

We provide 24/7 technical support. For guided tours and group rides, a backup vehicle with a qualified mechanic follows the convoy.

How cold does it get during the Winter Snow Leopard and Pangong tour?

Winter temperatures drop below -15°C to -25°C. We ensure warm accommodations, suitable heating options, and proper guidance for extreme cold weather preparation.
